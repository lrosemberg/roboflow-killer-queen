A FIELD GUIDE TO FLORIDA

Pick your crew.
Earn your wings.

♛

Queen

One queen per five teammates, chosen before the round; a full Classic team has one and a full XXL team has twelve. Hold jumpJUMP to fly with AI1; press S or Shifttap DIVE or pull the joystick down in the air to dive. Touch a gate to claim it for your team. Defeat opponents with directional contact from above. Lose all three lives and your queen is eliminated. Your team loses when every queen is out.

▣

Worker

Collect one Roboflow chip at a time from the supply piles. Bring it to your HQ to fill the vault. The target scales with everyone in the room, including bots: 12 chips per team at 10 players and 144 at 120. Supply piles scale too. The target locks when the countdown starts. Or bring it inside a neutral or friendly AI1 station and press Etap ACTION. Stay still while the installation completes: wings make you a warrior; lightning grants speed. Only workers collect data and ride the gator.

⚔

Warrior

Earn wings during the round, never in the lobby. Fly and defeat opponents through directional contact from above. You cannot carry data or ride the gator. When defeated, you return as a worker and lose upgrades.
